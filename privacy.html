PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy and demonstration scope

Last reviewed: November 21, 2026. This notice describes the new RegenExcalibur business and Conscience Engine demonstration pages; existing separately linked features may operate differently.

Browser-local evaluation

The Conscience Engine demo and project inquiry helper execute JavaScript in your browser. Neither page submits form data to a RegenExcalibur application server. Results are displayed in your browser only. Clicking “Export JSON” creates a downloadable file on your device. The inquiry helper generates a draft locally and can copy it to your clipboard. No draft information is transmitted through the RegenExcalibur website.

Use only general, non-sensitive information. Do not include confidential work, names, email addresses, phone numbers, credentials, regulated information or personally identifiable case details.

Hosting and linked services

Even static web hosting can generate normal access records handled by the hosting provider. Choosing to open the GitHub public inquiry form transfers you to GitHub, where your account details and any information you choose to submit are governed by GitHub's policies. A submitted GitHub issue is public. You must sign in and review the completed issue before submitting it. We do not represent GitHub issues as private messaging. This notice does not control external providers.

Claims and limitations

The interactive checker relies solely on self-reported checkbox answers. It cannot verify a claim, award certification, detect all risks, make professional judgments or grant authority. Real-world evaluation needs documented evidence and accountable human review.

Inquiries and communication

For public-safe project inquiries, use our project inquiry preparation page, then decide whether to submit the prepared text through GitHub. There is no verified private business inbox connected to this site yet. We do not list a personal mailbox as an official company contact.
