PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy and demonstration scope

Last reviewed: November 21, 2026. This notice describes the new RegenExcalibur business and Conscience Engine demonstration pages; existing separately linked features may operate differently.

Browser-local evaluation

The Conscience Engine demo executes JavaScript in your browser. Its form data is not submitted to a RegenExcalibur application server. Results are displayed in your browser only. Clicking “Export JSON” creates a downloadable file on your device; it is not uploaded by the demo.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information, confidential company materials, secrets, regulated information, or real-world consequential cases.

Hosting and linked services

Even static web hosting can generate normal access records handled by the hosting provider. External links—including GitHub and third-party reference standards—lead to sites that may process data under their own privacy notices. This notice does not control those services.

Claims and limitations

The interactive checker relies solely on self-reported checkbox answers. It cannot verify a claim, award certification, detect all risks, make professional judgments or grant authority. Real-world evaluation needs documented evidence and accountable human review.

Contact

For inquiries about this public demonstration, use the venture's existing GitHub contact point. No email address is represented here without verification.
