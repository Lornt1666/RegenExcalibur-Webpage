Intelligence with a conscience.
Decisions with evidence.

A proposed evaluation architecture for checking complex projects and proposed decisions for source quality, security, consent, potential harms, human agency and reversibility—before consequential actions occur.

Try the local demonstration ↗Explore the architecture
Research concept and demonstration, not certification. RGBP, AER and Tetrad-1 are proposed review roles within this framework. The interactive example operates entirely in your browser and does not conduct independent inspections, access external systems, or certify conduct.

COORDINATED ASSESSMENT

Different perspectives.
One accountable record.

Multiple independent challenge paths are designed to reduce blind spots. Review findings never substitute for authorized human decisions.

RGBP · ADVERSARIAL REVIEW

Red · Green · Blue · Purple

Red challenges failure modes and harmful consequences. Green tests feasibility and evidence. Blue examines security and privacy. Purple reconciles conflicts and identifies remaining uncertainty.

AER · EVIDENCE & ASSURANCE

Review the proof, not the promise.

Evidence provenance, reproducibility, contradictions and uncertainty are assessed before any result can be presented as validated. Records remain distinct from interpretations.

TETRAD-1 · SPECIALIST OPERATIONS

Govern the workflow.

Stages and handoffs can coordinate research, synthesis, analysis and review. Explicit policy checks block unauthorized execution, and consequential release remains human-controlled.

CONSCIENCE IS NOT A BLACK BOX

Non-negotiable
governance controls.

We propose fail-closed decisions for missing evidence and permissions, explicit traceability, and independent review—not self-declared authority.

Evidence before assertion

Claims preserve source, timestamp, methodology and confidence. A missing source remains unknown, not automatically false or true.

Security before execution

Tool grants are scoped to real authority. No agent can award itself broader access or bypass a policy hold.

Care for people and ecosystems

Stakeholder effects, accessibility, consent and environmental externalities belong in evaluation, not marketing decoration.

Humans retain responsibility

Review teams may challenge and recommend; only designated humans can approve consequential decisions within applicable law.

Methodology and safeguards are described in this demonstration. No third-party endorsement or certification is implied.
